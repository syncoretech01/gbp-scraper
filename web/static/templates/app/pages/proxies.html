Proxies are optional operational resources

The scraper works directly. Test only proxies you are authorised to use; stable residential/mobile networks and CAPTCHA solving are not bundled.

Import encrypted proxy pool

HTTP, HTTPS, and SOCKS5 URLs are accepted. Credentials are AES-GCM encrypted with a random key stored beside the database.

Paths, query strings, fragments, and unsupported schemes are rejected. Exact duplicates in the pool are skipped.

Health boundary

On-demand tests record Maps access, response latency, success/failure/block counts, cooldown, and automatic disable after repeated offline/authentication failures. Use "Retest disabled" on a pool to test up to 50 disabled proxies and re-enable the ones that are healthy again. Exit-IP country lookup and automatic background retesting are not performed to avoid an extra third-party service.
